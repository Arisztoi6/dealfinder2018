ESP32-S3 Standalone Diagnostic & Service Tool

Touchscreen battery diagnostics, Makita LXT service functions, Bluetooth Smart BMS support and built-in workshop utilities — all from one standalone device.

Important: Service and Smart BMS control functions can write data or settings to a battery management system. Only use a write/service function on the intended battery and do not disconnect the battery or power off the device while a write is in progress.

Introduction

Compatibility & Quick Start

The device can

  • Read supported Makita LXT battery information
  • Display pack voltage, individual cell groups and imbalance
  • Display temperature, cycle and identification data when available
  • Detect charger-lock, stored BMS faults and memory/checksum problems
  • Run guarded Makita service/recovery functions on supported batteries
  • Scan and communicate with supported Bluetooth Smart BMS systems
  • Provide workshop tools including Wi-Fi, BLE, sound meter, calculator and timer

The device cannot repair

  • Dead or internally shorted cells
  • Physically damaged cell connections or sense wiring
  • Blown fuses
  • Failed temperature sensors
  • Water, fire or impact damage
  • Burnt or physically damaged BMS electronics

First use

  1. Power on the device and allow the startup screen to finish.
  2. Choose STANDARD or PERSONALIZED setup. Personalized mode lets you add a name and choose the sound level.
  3. From the Home screen choose MAKITA LXT, SMART BMS, MULTITOOL or DEVICE.
  4. For Makita diagnostics, connect the supported battery and select SCAN BATTERY.
  5. For a Bluetooth BMS, wake the BMS/Bluetooth module and start with SMART BMS → AUTO DETECT.
Do not use the device on a battery that is swollen, leaking, excessively hot, burnt or mechanically damaged. A successful electronic read or reset does not prove that the cells are safe or that the battery has passed a load test.

Information available after a scan

  • Battery model and detected family
  • Nominal capacity and recorded charge cycles where available
  • Pack voltage
  • Individual cell-group voltages — 5-cell or supported 10-cell packs
  • Highest-to-lowest cell difference
  • Battery temperature sensors when available
  • Manufacturing/assembly information and ROM identity where supported
  • Stored fault, charger-lock and memory/checksum diagnostics
  • Additional counters and history data where the battery family provides them

Battery result status

StatusMeaning
PASSNo fault was detected in the data currently available to the device.
SERVICEA charger-lock or stored BMS condition was detected and a supported service function may be available.
CHECKA caution condition was detected, such as a low cell, higher imbalance or stored history that should be investigated.
HARDWAREThe readings indicate a likely physical battery problem such as a weak/dead group, sense issue, major imbalance or temperature-sensor fault.
UNKNOWNThe device did not receive enough valid live information to give a reliable result.
PASS is not a load test. It means the values and BMS information observed during the scan did not show a fault. Capacity and high-current performance still require appropriate battery testing.

Service Center

The available buttons depend on the detected battery type and condition. The current firmware includes:

  • SMART RECOVERY — automatic guarded recovery sequence for supported service conditions
  • DA04 ERROR RESET — targeted reset for supported batteries
  • SAFE FRAME UNLOCK — guarded frame-based unlock where compatible
  • LEGACY DEEP RECOVERY — advanced recovery for eligible older battery families
If the device reports HARDWARE, inspect and repair the physical battery fault before attempting BMS recovery. Service functions are intentionally blocked or limited on unsupported or unsafe conditions.

Wireless Diagnostics

Smart BMS

The Smart BMS section scans nearby Bluetooth devices and can automatically identify supported BMS families. For best results, start with AUTO DETECT.

  1. Make sure the battery/BMS is awake and Bluetooth is active.
  2. Open SMART BMS and select AUTO DETECT.
  3. Wait for the scan and select the correct BMS from the detected-device list.
  4. Review the overview and cell pages before opening any service page.
  5. Any supported write/control operation will require confirmation. After supported writes, the device rereads or verifies the result where the protocol allows it.

The Smart BMS menu contains additional BMS-family names for detection and compatibility work. Support varies by hardware generation and protocol. A family appearing in the menu does not guarantee that every read or write feature is available on every BMS.

ECBMS settings

ECBMS settings pages can read protection and operating parameters. Supported parameter changes show the old value and the proposed new value, require confirmation, authenticate with the BMS where required, then reread the BMS to verify the returned value.

Do not change protection thresholds simply because an editable field is available. Record the original values first and only use values suitable for the exact cell chemistry, series count, cell specification and battery design.

If Auto Detect does not find the BMS

  • Wake the battery/BMS and retry the scan
  • Move the tool closer to the battery
  • Close the manufacturer's phone app so it is not holding the BLE connection
  • Select the BMS family manually if known
  • Use MULTITOOL → BLE EXPLORER to confirm that the Bluetooth device is advertising

Power & Charging

Internal Battery, Charging & Screen Sleep

USB-C charging

Connect a suitable USB-C power source. The device automatically detects a charging session and shows the charging screen with the internal battery level and live voltage.

Charge complete

When the device detects a completed charge it shows FULL / CHARGE COMPLETE. The normal top-right battery icon also reflects the internal battery level.

Charging screen

When a charger is detected, the charging display appears automatically. After the short charging-status display period, the screen can switch off while charging continues. This is normal and reduces unnecessary display power use.

3-minute auto-off

With AUTO enabled, the display switches off after approximately three minutes without user activity. Touching the screen wakes the display and returns to the Home screen.

The percentage shown while charging is intentionally based on the pre-charge battery state and charging session, rather than simply treating the raised USB charging voltage as an immediate jump to 100%.
Help

Common Problems

Device does not turn on / screen is black
Charge the internal battery through USB-C. If the screen switched off through AUTO mode or the OFF button, touch the display to wake it. Try a different USB-C cable and power source if charging is not detected.
Makita battery is not detected
Remove and reconnect the battery, check the battery terminals/adapter, then scan again. The battery may be unsupported, deeply faulty or unable to provide valid BMS communication. Do not repeatedly attempt service writes if the basic scan cannot complete.
Result shows HARDWARE
A likely physical pack problem was detected. Investigate the reported condition — for example cell group, sense wiring, imbalance or thermistor — before using BMS recovery functions.
Service function is unavailable
The device only enables service functions when the detected battery family and current condition meet the firmware's safety checks. Some 10-cell/36V and special battery types are intentionally read-only.
Smart BMS scan finds nothing
Wake the BMS, move the tool closer, disconnect the manufacturer's phone app, then rescan. If necessary use BLE Explorer to confirm that the BMS is advertising.
Smart BMS connects but data is incomplete
BMS manufacturers often use multiple protocol generations under the same brand name. Retry the read, confirm the correct family selection and use Auto Detect where possible. Not every BMS generation exposes the same data or controls.
Touchscreen appears frozen
Allow any active battery read, BMS write or BLE operation to finish. If no operation is active and the device remains unresponsive, restart the device. Never interrupt a confirmed battery/BMS write unless absolutely necessary.
USB OBI bridge does not appear on the PC
Use a USB-C data cable rather than a charge-only cable. Open MAKITA LXT → TEST LAB → USB OBI before starting the PC software.
If a battery is damaged, overheated, swollen, wet, burnt, internally shorted or otherwise physically unsafe, stop using electronic reset/service functions. The device cannot make a physically unsafe battery safe.
