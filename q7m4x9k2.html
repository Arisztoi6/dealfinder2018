Official Instruction Manual

Makita Battery Service Tool

Standalone battery diagnostic and repair device designed for compatible Makita LXT 18V batteries.

Introduction

Compatibility & Quick Start

Compatible Batteries

  • BL1820
  • BL1830
  • BL1840
  • BL1850
  • BL1860
  • BL1890
  • BL18120
  • BL1820B
  • BL1830B
  • BL1840B
  • BL1850B
  • BL1860B
  • Other compatible LXT models may be supported.

Not Supported

  • Makita G-Series batteries
  • Makita XGT
  • Makita 36V LXT
  • 12V CXT batteries
  • Non-Makita batteries
  • Counterfeit batteries with incompatible electronics

The Repair Function Cannot Fix

  • Dead cells
  • Weak cells
  • Open circuit connections
  • Blown fuses
  • Failed temperature sensors
  • Water damage
  • Burnt or damaged BMS electronics

The Device Can

  • Read battery information
  • Display pack voltage and cell voltages
  • Display battery temperature
  • Detect charger lock conditions
  • Detect memory errors
  • Attempt repair of supported charger lock faults
  • Display advanced battery memory information

Quick Start

  1. Connect battery.
  2. Switch ON plastic power switch.
  3. Press metal button.
  4. Select READ BATTERY.
  5. Review battery information.
  6. If battery is reported BLOCKED, select REPAIR LOCK.
  7. Read battery again to confirm status.

Top Metal Button Functions

Short press: Move through menus and pages.

Long press (2 sec): Select a menu item or return to the previous menu.

Power off press (5 sec): Turn the device off.

Debug menu press (20 sec): Open the advanced debug menu.

Important
Do not remove the battery or switch off the device while a repair operation is in progress. Interrupting a repair procedure may leave the battery memory in an unknown state.
Main Menu

Device Menu & Battery Information

READ BATTERY

Reads battery information directly from the battery.

This should normally be the first option used.

REPAIR LOCK

Attempts to repair supported charger lock conditions.

Only available when the battery is detected as blocked.

VIEW DATA

Displays information already read from the battery.

POWER OFF

Turns the device off.

USED: Moderate cycle count.

Repair Function

If a battery is blocked:

  1. Select REPAIR LOCK.
  2. Wait for the repair process to finish.
  3. Read the battery again.

REPAIR OK: The battery lock was successfully removed.

REPAIR FAIL: The battery could not be repaired automatically.

Understanding Common Faults

Charger Lock
Battery has been electronically blocked; repair may be possible.

Stored Fault
Battery has recorded a fault condition; repair may be possible.

Memory Error
Checksum values do not match. Battery memory corruption detected.

No Lock Detected

Battery memory appears healthy.

If the battery still does not operate correctly, the problem is likely a hardware issue such as:

  • Weak cell group
  • Failed cell
  • Broken connection
  • Failed thermistor
  • Damaged BMS electronics
  • Internal wiring fault
Power & Charging

Internal Battery, Charging & Storage

The repair function cannot repair hardware faults.

Auto Power Off

The device automatically powers off after approximately 3 minutes of inactivity to conserve the internal battery.

Internal Battery and Charging

The device contains an internal rechargeable lithium-polymer battery.

Charging the Device

The device is charged through the USB-C charging connector.

The internal charging circuit automatically controls the charging process.

Charge Complete

When charging is complete:

  • The moving dot stops.
  • The display switches off.
  • The battery remains fully charged and ready for use.

Charging Status

When a USB-C charger is connected, the display automatically shows the cell voltage and charging status.

As charging continues, the voltage gradually increases towards approximately 4.20V.

Charging Animation

After approximately 6 seconds, the display switches to power-saving mode.

The screen becomes blank except for a moving dot.

The moving dot indicates:

  • Charger connected
  • Charging in progress
  • Device operating normally

Push Button LED

The illuminated push button serves as a device activity indicator.

Normal Operation: LED OFF.

Battery Reading: LED may flash briefly during communication with the battery.

Repair Operation: LED remains illuminated while repair procedures are being performed.

Do not disconnect the battery during a repair operation.

Low Battery Warning

If the internal battery becomes very low:

  • Battery icon will show a low charge level.
  • Battery communication may become unreliable.
  • The device may switch off automatically.

Recharge the device before continuing use.

Internal Battery Indicator

A battery icon is displayed in the top-right corner of the screen.

Four Bars: Internal battery fully charged.

Three Bars: Good charge level.

Two Bars: Partially discharged.

One Bar: Low battery. Recharge soon.

Empty Battery Symbol: Battery critically low. Recharge immediately.

Typical Runtime

The built-in 1500mAh Li-Po battery typically provides many hours of normal operation between charges.

Switch off the device with the power plastic switch if you don't use it, otherwise the device is still running except the screen.

Reading Time

After selecting READ BATTERY, the device may take several seconds to communicate with the battery and retrieve information.

Typical reading time is between 5 and 10 seconds depending on the battery model and condition.

During this time, do not press any buttons or disconnect the battery.

When the reading process is complete, the battery information pages will automatically become available.

Storage

For long-term storage:

  • Switch OFF with main plastic switch.
  • Store with approximately 50–80% charge.
  • Recharge every 6–12 weeks.
  • Store in a cool, dry location.

This helps maximize battery life.

Troubleshooting

Common Problems

Device Does Not Turn On

Possible Causes:

  • Internal battery discharged
  • Power switch OFF

No Battery Detected

Possible Causes:

  • Battery not fully inserted
  • Dirty battery terminals
  • Unsupported battery
  • Damaged battery electronics

Solutions:

  • Remove and reconnect the battery
  • Check battery terminal condition
  • Try another compatible Makita battery

Battery Read Fails

Possible Causes:

  • Battery communication fault
  • Unsupported battery
  • Counterfeit battery
  • Damaged battery memory
  • Internal battery charge too low

Solutions:

  • Read the battery again
  • Recharge the device
  • Try another battery
  • Check battery condition

No Data Appears After Selecting READ BATTERY

Possible Causes:

  • Battery is still being scanned
  • Communication problem

Solutions:

  • Wait at least 10 seconds
  • Do not press any buttons during reading
  • Retry the reading process

Repair Fails

Possible Causes:

  • Unsupported lock condition
  • Memory corruption
  • Hardware fault inside battery

Solutions:

  • Read battery information again
  • Check the reported fault reason
  • Hardware repairs may be required

Battery Charges Normally, but Tool Reports No Lock

Possible Cause: Battery fault is hardware related.

Possible Hardware Faults:

  • Weak cell group
  • Failed cell
  • Blown fuse
  • Failed thermistor
  • Damaged BMS electronics
  • Internal wiring fault

REPAIR LOCK Not Available

Possible Cause: Battery is not detected as blocked.

Solution:

  • Read the battery first
  • The repair function only appears when a supported lock condition is detected

Device Does Not Charge

Possible Causes:

  • Faulty USB-C cable
  • Faulty USB charger
  • Charging connector damaged

Solutions:

  • Try a different USB-C cable
  • Try a different charger
  • Inspect the charging connector

Screen Frozen or Unresponsive

Solution:

  • Switch OFF the main power switch
  • Wait 5 seconds
  • Switch ON the main power switch
  • Restart the device
The repair function cannot repair hardware faults.
