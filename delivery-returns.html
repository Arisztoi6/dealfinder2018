Cancellation for UK consumer orders

For standard non-personalised goods bought online, UK consumers normally have 14 days after receiving the goods to tell us they wish to cancel, without giving a reason. You then have a further 14 days to return the goods. Contact us by email stating your order reference and your decision to cancel. We will provide return instructions. You may use the cancellation wording below.

For a change-of-mind cancellation, you normally pay the direct cost of return postage. We will refund the product price and standard outbound delivery charge within the statutory period, subject to the applicable rules concerning returned goods. We may make a lawful deduction where goods have been handled beyond what is necessary to inspect them.

Faulty or misdescribed goods are covered by your statutory consumer rights, which are not limited by this cancellation policy or any additional product warranty. If your item is faulty, contact us so we can arrange an appropriate resolution.
